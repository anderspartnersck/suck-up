A Castle Killscreen arcade game by Anders & Partners LLC. Free, in your
browser, nothing to install.

Two alien pilots race to abduct more than the other from a scrolling night farm
outside Towson, Maryland. Cows, people, the odd power-up. It isn't co-op. You
both work the same field, and the MILITARY RESPONSE meter along the bottom is
shared, so whatever your rival stirs up comes for you as well.

(Near Towson. Not in it. Dave will correct you.)

## How to play

Fly over a target and hold **BEAM** to pull it up. **WARBLE** phases you
through incoming fire for a moment, and a well-timed one sends the shot back.
Farmers shoot back. On County Road 9, so do the cops.

**On a phone,** turn it sideways. A column down the left edge holds two
buttons: WARBLE on top, BEAM below it, both for one thumb.

- Drag anywhere on the game with your other thumb to fly. The saucer moves
  with your thumb from where it already is, so your thumb never covers it.
- Outside of play, tap the game to start or confirm, and swipe to move
  through menus.
- The ⇄ LEFTY chip (shown outside of play) moves the buttons to the right
  side. `?hand=right` on the link does the same.
- The phone is one player. Two players need a keyboard.

**On a computer:**

- Move the mouse and the saucer follows your cursor. Hold left-click to beam,
  right-click to warble.
- On the keyboard it's WASD or the arrow keys to fly, **Z** to beam, **X** to
  warble. Space starts a game (and also beams), and M mutes.
- **Esc** (or P) pauses: RESUME, RESTART or QUIT TO TITLE. On a pad it's
  START; on a phone, the ❚❚ PAUSE chip in the top corner.
- A second player can join on the same keyboard. When the game asks DO YOU
  THINK YOU ARE ALONE?, press `;` to jump in, then fly with IJKL, beam with M
  and warble with the comma key. M stops muting once two people are playing.

## The three cabinets

All three run the 3D edition: beams with real volume and a contest you can read,
saucer light on the ground, moon shadows, 3D jets and tanks, 3D trees, water
tower and hay bales across the farm, depth behind the skyline, and the gnome army lit for the Gunpowder Horror.

[HYSCORE FOREST][play] is the arcade cab, and the one to start with. Attract
reel, a full run, then continue or begin again. No stage select. On the web the
rounds run long and you can pause. [The coin-op version][coinop] plays the way the
real cabinet does: the original short rounds, no pause, and no quitting mid-run.

[BLACKSITE][story] is the same game, but it tracks your progress: clear a zone
and the next one opens, and it drops you into the furthest one you have reached.
There is no level select, it just continues. That progress lasts for the session
and resets when you refresh.

[COLEMAN'S ORIGINAL][testcab] is Blacksite with every zone already open. Use it
to jump straight to the later stages.

Sound sliders are in the corner of the screen on all of them (on a computer).

### The deck

THE DECK mounts a cabinet over its own control panel, and the panel is live. The
BEAM and WARBLE buttons light when they fire, the sticks lean the way the saucer
is flying, and the MILITARY RESPONSE strip fills as the heat climbs. You can
click the buttons too, and they play exactly as the keys do.

Each cabinet brings its own deck, because they are not the same machine:

- [HYSCORE FOREST deck][deck]: the painted arcade panel
- [BLACKSITE deck][deckstory]: the jailbroken one
- [COLEMAN'S ORIGINAL deck][deckog]: Blacksite's panel with every zone open

### The vault

Press X (or right-click) at the attract screen before you start and you'll arm
DEFCON 1, which is one credit with no continues. Finish a run on that and the
cheats vault unlocks. It holds every level, Dave's Backyard, the Military Base,
Claw & Order, the Champion Lap, the full run, DEFCON 1 with unlimited
continues, god mode, The Gunpowder Horror, three codes, and the attract film on
its own.

The arcade cab has no menus, so the vault only opens on the Blacksite cab:
earn it there, then press C at the attract screen. Because nothing is saved
(see below), it has to be earned in the same tab you open it in. On Coleman's
Original it's already open.

Progress, scores and the vault last as long as the tab is open and reset on
refresh, the way a cabinet forgets your game when the power cycles. The web
game saves nothing on your device and sends nothing anywhere.
